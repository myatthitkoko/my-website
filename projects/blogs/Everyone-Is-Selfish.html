Is Everyone Selfish?

A few days ago, I had an interesting conversation with a classmate after my Physics class ended. It all started with my claim that "Everyone is inherently selfish" so you might as well be honest about it rather than masking yourself in altruism. This triggered an hour long discussion and debate in the student parking lot.
