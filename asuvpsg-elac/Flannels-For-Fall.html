Flannels For Fall | Southeast Campus

Happy Holidays! My name is Myat Thit Ko Ko. I serve as the Vice President of South Gate (VPSG) of Associated Student Union (ASU) of East Los Angeles College (ELAC) for 2026-2027 academic year. For Thanksgiving this year, I am providing giveaway flannels for students at Southeast Campus. I am also taking this opportunity to collaborate with ELAC Foundation and promote the Husky Closet program resources for students.

Event Date: Tuesday, Nov 24 2026

Event Time: 4 pm - 7 pm

Event starts from now.

  • How do I get a flannel?
  • How many flannels will be provided?
  • What sizes and colors are available?
  • What is the Husky Closet Program?
  • Access for Southeast students

How do I get a flannel?

Just show up with the event poster. Don't forget to bring your Student ID to check in at the table.

How many flannels will be provided?

An order of 30 flannels in total has been placed. Since the giveaway items are limited, we encourage all students to show up as early as 4 o'clock to bring a flannel home.

What sizes and colors are available?

We've got green plaid shirts and brown plaid shirts. There are 15 of each color. Each color has 5 medium, 5 large, and 5 extra-large sizes.

What is the Husky Closet Program?

Husky Closet provides a variety of clothing items such as hats, footwear, and suits for students at free of charge to help you feel confident and prepared for school, job interviews, internships, career fairs, and professional opportunities. Just like a shopping experience, students could bring their selections at the check-out counter for up to 5 items per week. It is open daily from 11 am to 1 pm at E3-255 at East Campus.

I am a student at Southeast Campus. How can I access the Husky Closet?

You could take the shuttle to the East Campus. Campus map has been provided below for your convenience.
