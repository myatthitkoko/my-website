Meet the Vice President of South Gate (VPSG)

Myat Thit Ko Ko

Myat Thit Ko Ko is a student leader majoring in Computer Science at East Los Angeles College. He has worked toward his goal of transferring to a four-year university while balancing rigorous coursework, student leadership, and professional responsibilities.

Leadership and Career

Beyond the classroom, Ko serves as Vice President of South Gate in ELAC's ASU for 2026-2027 academic year. In this capacity, he represents student interests, brings campus concerns to student government discussions, participates in organizational decisions, and coordinates with campus personnel and community partners to support student engagement. His responsibilities have provided opportunities to develop leadership, advocate for fellow students, and contribute to the broader campus community.

His interest in technology extends beyond academic instruction. Through independent software development, Ko has built and maintained his personal website to have a digital presence. He has also developed practial webpages such as Temporary Southeast Campus Parking Info to meet the immediate needs of the organization. These projects reflect his interest in developing practical solutions that address real-world needs.

Academic Background

Throughout 2026, Ko achieved a 4.0 GPA across his academic terms and earned recognition on the Dean's List. His coursework has focused on developing a strong foundation in computer science, including data structures, object-oriented programming, mathematics, and physics. For Fall 2027, he recently applied as a transfer student to four University of California (UC) campuses: UCLA, UC Berkeley, UC Irvine, and UC San Diego.

Hospitality

Alongside his academic and technical pursuits, Ko maintains professional experience through employment at Kura Sushi and 24 Carrots. At Kura Sushi, he assists guests in navigating technology-assisted dining systems, troubleshooting ordering difficulties, and resolving technical issues. His experience at 24 Carrots has involved coordinating with catering teams, executing Banquet Event Orders, and adapting to the demands of diverse events and venues. Together, these roles have strengthened his communication, adaptability, and ability to work collaboratively in fast-paced environments.

Ko represents the intersection of academic discipline, practical experience, and student leadership. As he prepares for the next stage of his education, he hopes to continue building solutions that connect technical innovation with everyday needs.
